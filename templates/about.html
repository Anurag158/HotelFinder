About HotelFinder

Discover the right stay for every journey.

Welcome to HotelFinder

HotelFinder is a hotel discovery and recommendation platform designed to make finding and exploring hotels easier. Our goal is to help users discover suitable accommodation by providing useful hotel information in one convenient place.

Whether you are exploring hotels in a new city or comparing different accommodation options, HotelFinder helps you make more informed decisions through organized hotel data.

What We Offer

1. Easy Hotel Search

Search for hotels using hotel names, cities, countries, addresses, and property types. Use filters to narrow down results according to star ratings, currencies, and historical price information.

2. Hotel Recommendations

Explore recommended hotels based on available dataset information, including star ratings and valid historical rate records.

3. Detailed Hotel Information

Explore hotel locations, property types, room information, amenities, ratings, and other available details.

4. Price Information

Compare historical average, minimum, and maximum observed hotel rates to understand the price information available in the dataset.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify hotel discovery by making hotel information easier to access, understand, and compare. We aim to provide a user-friendly platform that helps travellers explore accommodation options efficiently.

Our Commitment

We are committed to presenting hotel information in a clear, organized, and accessible way. We continuously aim to improve the hotel discovery experience through useful features and data-driven functionality.

Data transparency: Hotel rate information displayed on this platform comes from historical observations collected during 2023–2025. These values are not live booking prices or current availability.
